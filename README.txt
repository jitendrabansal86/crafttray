# Your Brand — Static Catalogue Website

## Pages
- index.html — Home
- products.html — Product catalogue with category filters
- about.html — About/brand story
- contact.html — Contact details + static enquiry form

## How to customize
1. Replace "Your Brand" and "YB" with your brand name/logo.
2. Replace placeholder image boxes with your own product/brand images.
3. Edit product names, descriptions and categories in products.html.
4. Edit About and Contact content.
5. Open index.html in a browser to preview.

No shopping cart, checkout, backend or database is included.
